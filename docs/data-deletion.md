# CupWhisper — Data Deletion Request

CupWhisper is developed by The Architect of Tiles. This page explains how
you can request deletion of your data associated with the CupWhisper app.

## How to Request Deletion

1. Send an email to **masal230718@gmail.com** with the subject line
   "CupWhisper Data Deletion Request."
2. If possible, include the approximate date you first used the app and
   which storyteller/language you used most, to help us locate your
   anonymous account data faster.
3. We will process your request and confirm by email once your data has
   been deleted, typically within 14 days.

## What Data Is Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, the following data associated with
your anonymous account identifier will be permanently deleted from our
systems:

- Your reading history (text of past AI-generated readings)
- Your saved language and storyteller preferences
- Your anonymous account identifier itself

## What Data Is Not Retained in the First Place

CupWhisper does not permanently store the photos you take of your coffee
cup. Photos are processed in real time to generate your reading and are
not saved on our servers afterward, so there is nothing to delete in
that category.

## Data Retained After Deletion

Aggregated, anonymized analytics data (collected via Firebase Analytics)
that cannot be tied back to an individual user may be retained for
product improvement purposes, consistent with Google's own data
retention practices for Firebase Analytics.

For more details, see our full [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).
