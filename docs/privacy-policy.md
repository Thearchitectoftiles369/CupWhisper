# Privacy Policy for CupWhisper

**Last updated: August 2026**

CupWhisper ("the App," "we," "us") is an entertainment app that generates
AI-powered, playful coffee-cup readings. This Privacy Policy explains what
information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Coffee cup photos.** When you take or select a photo of your coffee cup,
it is sent to our backend server and processed by Google's Gemini AI to
generate your reading. Photos are processed in real time and are not
permanently stored on our servers after your reading is generated.

**Anonymous account identifier.** The App uses Firebase Anonymous
Authentication to assign your device a unique, anonymous identifier. This
identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personally
identifying information unless you choose to provide it.

**Reading history and preferences.** We store the text of your past
readings, your selected storyteller, and your selected language,
associated with your anonymous identifier, so you can keep a history of
your readings within the App.

**Usage analytics.** We use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to
understand how the App is used, in order to improve it. This may include
device information, general usage patterns, and app performance data.
Analytics data is not used to personally identify you.

## How We Use Your Information

- To generate your AI-powered coffee reading.
- To let you view your past readings within the App.
- To remember your language and storyteller preferences.
- To understand app usage and fix problems, using aggregated analytics.

## Third-Party Services

CupWhisper relies on the following third-party services, each governed by
its own privacy policy:

- **Google Gemini API** - processes your cup photo and generates the
  reading content and voice narration.
- **Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics) - Google Cloud
  services used to run the App's backend features.
- **Google Cloud Run** - hosts the App's server-side processing.

## Data Retention

Reading history is retained until you request deletion or uninstall the
App. Since accounts are anonymous, deletion requests should be made by
contacting us with your in-app data if you wish to have your history
removed from our systems.

## Children's Privacy

CupWhisper is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the
relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Entertainment Purposes Only

CupWhisper's readings are generated by AI for entertainment purposes
only. They are not real predictions, professional advice, or a substitute
for guidance from a qualified expert.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
