# ☕ CupWhisper

> Every cup has a story.

CupWhisper is an AI-powered entertainment app inspired by the tradition of Turkish coffee reading.

The app does not predict the future.

Instead, it creates artistic stories inspired by coffee cup patterns through unique cultural voices.

---

## Mission

Create beautiful, respectful and entertaining storytelling experiences inspired by coffee traditions from around the world.

---

## Current Status

Phase 1 — Foundation

Project is currently under active development.

---

## Core Principles

- Entertainment First
- Respect Cultural Traditions
- AI Transparency
- Privacy by Design
- Beautiful User Experience
